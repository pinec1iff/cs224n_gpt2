Beshrew that heart that makes my heart to groan
For that deep wound it gives my friend and me.
Is 't not enough to torture me alone,
When on that heart which hath no hate doth my name plead
Against that which is thy proud name praised,

Thou blind fool, Love, what dost thou to mine eyes
That they behold and see not what they see?
They know what beauty is, see where it lies,
They love what I love and not all that I write;
I am rich, you poor love me rich.
All those men that love them which write
Thou, being rich, as the stars do increase
Called poor, are poor still and poor still.
  Be the good to me thou write'st for that my eye doth see,
  Mine to thee do I make thee worth in me .

When my love swears that she is made of truth
I do believe her though I know she lies,
That she might think me some untutored youth,
While she myself is truth's worth--a true being,
Hearing so many false lies from all who tell
The common error. And as she swears
That you were the greatest in rank with those who tell,
She holds no rank more for more than those you do love,
Than she can tell those falsehoods even worse,
The proud pride of truth for false praise:
-- "But how was this told, that by the truth I win?"
